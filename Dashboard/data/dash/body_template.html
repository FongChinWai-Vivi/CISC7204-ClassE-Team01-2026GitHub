1 · Long-run monthly visitor arrivals, 2015-2026

Pandemic years are drawn in red and cannot be read as normal demand; the shaded band is the 36-month estimation window used in every correlation reported below.

2 · Arrivals and bus passenger trips, monthly

Arrivals recover year by year, but the ridership line on the right axis does not keep pace - the coupling weakens as the market grows.

3 · Bus trips per visitor, quarterly

The headline ratio falls every quarter: each visitor is served by fewer scheduled trips than the quarter before.

4 · Visitor arrivals and the hotel occupancy rate

Rooms fill as the city fills: the occupancy rate on the right axis tracks visitor arrivals on the left, month by month, so the hotel stock is doing the work of absorbing the crowd.

5 · Occupancy against bus trips per visitor

Each point is one month and the colour is the year. Occupancy pushes to the right across 2023-2026 while ridership per visitor slides down: the rooms fill up, but the bus network does not carry proportionally more.

6 · Who fills the rooms: hotel guests by hotel class

Monthly guest counts split by hotel star rating, straight from the DSEC hotel survey. The 5-star tier dominates the stack, so capacity that ignores it is capacity aimed at a minority of the hotel base.

7 · The long occupancy record, 2014-2026

The full DSEC monthly occupancy series: the pandemic collapse, the 2023-2026 climb, and how far the market now sits above the 2019 mean before the buses are planned for it.

8 · Supply-demand mismatch across the three zones

Demand share is compared with the share of scheduled departures and of bus stops. A zone where the demand bar is taller than the supply bars is under-supplied.

9 · Visitor headcount carried per scheduled departure

The cleanest single measure of load: how many visitor-hours of demand sit behind each scheduled departure in the zone. Hover for the supply/demand ratio.

10 · Demand and scheduled supply by hour of day

The red line is the visitor demand share for the current filter; the blue line is the scheduled departure share. Bars are the gap in percentage points - red bars are hours where demand runs ahead of supply.

Hour focus: 12:00 - the heatmap column and the bars of this chart follow the hour-of-day slider.

11 · The 24 DSEC statistical districts

The Year / Zone / District filters above drive this map (colours are recomputed for the selected year); pick a Zone or a district with the top filters to spotlight it. Click a district here to filter views 8, 9, 10 and 12.

12 · District by hour-of-day profile

Each row is rescaled to that district's own daily total, so a small district and Cotai can be read side by side. Click a row to filter.

13 · Seasonality of arrivals, 2023-2025

Index on the 2023-2025 monthly mean; red bars are the flagged peak months. Capacity planning that ignores this shape under-supplies the peaks.

14 · Golden Week daily intensity

Daily arrivals inside the 1st October Golden Week expressed as a multiple of that month's average day. The shape of the peak is what the bus schedule has to absorb.

15 · What shifted in 2026

2026 year-to-date against the 2023-2025 baseline: the demand keeps tilting towards Cotai, and the hour-of-day shape barely moves.

How to read this dashboard. Every figure is computed at build time from the cleaned panels in data/, which are the files committed in data_processed/ of FongChinWai-Vivi/CISC7204-ClassE-Team01-2026GitHub. Headcount is a stock summed over the panel (2023-01 to 2026-09) and is comparable between districts, never across publications. Density is the only column comparable across district sizes. Supply is stop-level scheduled departures; stops outside the 24 polygons are excluded, not snapped. Demand-side sources: MGTO Macao Tourism Data Plus (district x hour), DSEC visitor arrivals, hotel occupancy and hotel guests. Supply-side sources: DSAT monthly and quarterly ridership and timetable releases, data.gov.mo public bus route and stop export. 2026 is partial in every view that shows it.
Hotel block: hotel_occ_monthly_2023_2026.csv, hotel_guests_by_star_2023_2026.csv and hotel_occ_history_2014_2025.csv, copied from Dataset/ of the same team repository (hotel occupancy total, guests by hotel class and the long 2014-2026 occupancy record).
Built with Plotly.js inside a single offline HTML file - no server and no network connection required.
